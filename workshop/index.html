    Plan B

    • No Wi-Fi: phone hotspot, or the laptop alone: pnpm dev and its webcam. Run Pre-flight while you still have a connection: the libraries then come from the cache.
    • No camera: the replay photos: the "backup" demos above, or More → Sample photos in the app. node replay/run.js <game> needs no browser at all.
    • Pages not live yet: show the owner demo, https://jannistriestocode.github.io/board-vision/; meanwhile on the laptop pnpm dev. Pages: repo → Settings → Pages → Source: GitHub Actions, then push.
    • Phone too slow: the laptop with pnpm dev and its webcam, or the replay photos on the owner demo (the "backup" demos above).
